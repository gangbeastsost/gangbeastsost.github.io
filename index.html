About This Player

A web-based music player for the Gang Beasts Official Soundtrack, featuring tracks by doseone & Bob Larder and custom fan-made modded maps.

This player supports gapless looping, shuffle mode, deep linking to specific tracks, and downloads.

Sharing Tracks: Click the button next to any track title to copy a shareable link that embeds properly into Discord and opens directly to that song.
